HelpBuoy Desk is the app for techies: see the screen of a computer you support and use its mouse and keyboard, after its user allows it.

The person you help runs the free HelpBuoy app on Windows, Linux or macOS. HelpBuoy Desk lists their computers, shows which are online, and opens a session when you click Connect.

How it works
1. Run a server: leave the address empty to run one on this computer, or connect to a HelpBuoy server your helpdesk runs.
2. The user types your server address in HelpBuoy and sends you the key it shows, for example in a chat. Paste it under Register computer, once.
3. When they need help, they open HelpBuoy. Click Connect; they allow it, and you see their screen.

Made for helping
• Sessions open in tabs, so you can help several people at once.
• Fit the screen to the window, show it 1:1 or resize the window to it, or go full screen.
• Switch between monitors, and choose Auto, Sharp or Fast for the picture.
• Send Win, Alt+Tab and other keys the local system would take.
• Group computers per customer and filter the list.
• Add techies, your colleagues, so they can help the same computers.

Safe for the people you help
• Every session needs the user's approval, and Esc on their side ends it.
• A session carries only the screen and your mouse and keyboard: no file transfer, no command line.
• Users see the company name and phone number of your helpdesk.
• Self-hosted: the connection goes through your own server, over HTTPS or plain SSH.

HelpBuoy Desk is free and open source (MIT license) and also runs on Linux and macOS.

More about HelpBuoy, and how to set up a helpdesk: www.helpbuoy.com
