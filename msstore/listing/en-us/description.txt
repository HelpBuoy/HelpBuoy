HelpBuoy lets a techie you trust help you on your PC. They see your screen and use your mouse and keyboard, but only after you allow it.

This is the app for the person who receives help. Your techie or helpdesk uses HelpBuoy Desk and a HelpBuoy server; all you need is the server address they give you.

How it works
1. Open HelpBuoy and type the server address your techie gave you.
2. HelpBuoy shows a key for this PC. Copy it and send it to your techie, who registers this PC once.
3. When you need help, open HelpBuoy. Your techie asks to connect, and you choose Allow or Not now.

You stay in control
• Every session asks for your approval first.
• Press Esc at any time to end the session and take back control.
• A session carries only your screen and the techie's mouse and keyboard: no file transfer, no command line.
• HelpBuoy shows who is helping you: the name and phone number of your helpdesk.
• Lock this PC to your helpdesk, so other servers can only connect after your helpdesk allows them.

Works where others don't
• HelpBuoy connects over HTTPS, so it gets through networks that only allow web traffic, and it uses the proxy settings of Windows.
• No account needed: HelpBuoy only connects to the server your helpdesk gives you.

HelpBuoy is free and open source (MIT license) and also runs on macOS and Linux.
